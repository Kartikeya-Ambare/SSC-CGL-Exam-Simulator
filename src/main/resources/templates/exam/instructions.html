General Instructions
  • The examination consists of 100 questions divided into 4 sections.
  • Total duration is 60 minutes (3600 seconds). The timer starts the moment you click "Begin Test".
  • The exam will be automatically submitted when the time expires.
  • Each question has 4 options with only one correct answer.
  • You can navigate to any question using the question palette on the right side.
  • Use "Save & Next" to save your answer and move to the next question.
  • Use "Mark for Review" to mark a question and review it later.
  • Use "Clear Response" to deselect your chosen answer.
  • You can revisit and change answers at any time before submitting.
  • Click "Submit Exam" when you are ready to finish.
  • Refreshing the page will not reset the timer — your progress is auto-saved.
  • Do not use the browser back button during the exam.
